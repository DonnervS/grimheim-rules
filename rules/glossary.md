# Glossary

*Every term the rules lean on, in one place. Each entry gives the short meaning and points to the section where the full rule lives.*

## Terms A to Z

Terms are listed alphabetically. Abbreviations used on cards and in profiles stand in brackets after the term. Underlined terms anywhere in the book lead here; the links inside an entry lead on to the full rule.

**Jump to:** A · B · C · D · E · F · G · H · I · L · M · N · O · P · R · S · T · V · W

**Actions (ACT)**

The number of actions a model can perform during its activation. Each action costs 1 ACT unless its entry says otherwise. See Actions.

**Arcane Energy (AE)**

Energy that powers spells, generated by rolling 2D6 when a model performs CAST A SPELL. See Arcane & Divine Powers.

**Attack Die**

A D6 rolled for a weapon's ATK. It succeeds on the weapon's HTV or higher; a 6 is a critical success, a 1 always fails. In Close Combat a successful Attack Die is spent to Strike or to Parry. See Close Combat and Ranged Combat.

**Attacker**

The model declaring a FIGHT or SHOOT action. See Close Combat and Ranged Combat.

**Base Contact**

Bases touching or within 1″ of each other. Required for Close Combat. See Movement.

**Battle Condition**

An optional effect on the whole battle, agreed before the game. See Battle Conditions.

**Block Die**

A bonus die from Shields or other rules, rolled alongside the Attack Dice in Close Combat. It is spent only to block an enemy success of equal or lower value. See Close Combat.

**Body Type**

Character Builder category that determines the physical characteristics of a model. See Custom Character Builder.

**Character Builder**

The system for creating custom models with point costs. See Custom Character Builder.

**Class**

A category of Traits that defines special abilities or roles. See Custom Character Builder.

**Critical Injury**

An injury caused by a critical hit, rolled on the Critical Injuries Table. See Vitality.

**Critical Success**

A roll of 6. It often triggers additional effects. See Dice and Notation.

**Defeated**

A model at 0 or fewer Vitality. It is removed from the game. See Vitality.

**Defender**

The target of an attack action. See Close Combat and Ranged Combat.

**Defense Dice**

The three D6 a model rolls to defend against Ranged Attacks; Stalwart adds one. See Ranged Combat.

**Deployment Zone**

The area where players set up their models at the start of the game. See Setup.

**Divine Energy (DE)**

Energy generated by rolling 2D6 when a model performs INVOCATION. See Arcane & Divine Powers.

**Equipment Table**

The D66 table that determines the loot found when plundering. See Equipment.

**Faction**

The army a model belongs to: Imperial, Undead, Primordial or Tribes; a warband that mixes factions is a Mercenary warband. Ratlings are a Body Type of the Primordial faction. See Choose a Faction.

**Fatal Damage/Wounds**

Damage that cannot be blocked or saved: no Block Die, no Defense roll, no Armor Save. See Vitality.

**Fate Die/Dice**

A special pool of dice rolled at the start of the round and spent during the Action Phase. See Fate and Turns.

**Fate Modifier**

Faction-specific rules for Fate Dice, with point costs. See Fate.

**Fate Phase**

The phase at the start of the round in which Fate Dice are rolled. See Turns.

**Garrison**

Models the Defender activates in some Stronghold Missions; not part of the warband, no kill VP. See Mission Rules.

**Gold**

The Campaign currency, equivalent to point values (20 Gold = 1 VP; Underdog 30 Gold). See Campaign Rules.

**Grimheim Hub**

The camp between Campaign missions, with various locations. See Campaign Rules.

**Hidden**

Status gained by performing HIDE while in Cover and outside every enemy's Line of Sight. A Hidden model cannot be targeted by enemies that are not in Base Contact with it. It ends when the model moves or acts in a way that is not compatible with Hide. See Sight & Targeting.

**Hit Value (HTV)**

The target number on a D6 for a successful attack. A lower HTV is better. Rules change it only as "HTV improved by N" (lower the target, never below 2+) or "HTV worsened by N" (raise the target, never above 6+). See Dice and Notation.

**Hold**

A player holds an objective if more of their models control it than enemy models do. See Mission Rules.

**Iconic Warband**

A pre-built warband from the faction chapters. See Choose a Faction.

**Initiative Phase**

The phase at the start of the round in which a dice roll determines the order of the players. See Turns.

**Line of Sight (LoS)**

The sight line between models, used for attacks and effects. See Sight & Targeting.

**Lore (Arcane/Divine)**

A collection of spells or divine powers for a faction. See Arcane & Divine Powers.

**Magic Scroll**

A one-use spell without the risk of an energy roll. See Equipment.

**Mission**

A scenario with specific objectives and victory conditions. See Missions.

**Mission Map**

A Mission that gives its own table layout; the players only add scatter terrain. See Setup Terms.

**Model**

An individual miniature in the game, with its own stats. See The Model Card.

**Movement (MOV)**

The movement distance in inches per action. See Movement.

**Normal Success**

A roll result equal to or higher than the target value, but not a 6. See Dice and Notation.

**NPC**

A non-player character. NPCs belong to neither player and act in the NPC Step at the start of each turn. See Mission Rules.

**Objective Area**

A central House, Hill or Object no more than 8″ across that is held instead of a token. See Mission Rules.

**Objective Marker**

A terrain marker for Victory Points. See Setup.

**Overwatch**

The state after an OVERWATCH action. It enables Reactions. See Reactions.

**Parry**

Spending a successful Attack Die in Close Combat to cancel an enemy success. A normal success cancels a normal success; a critical success cancels any success. See Close Combat.

**Permanent Wound**

A lasting injury from the Campaign aftermath. See Campaign Rules.

**Points (pts)**

The cost of models and equipment in warband building. See Points.

**Range (RNG)**

The maximum distance of a ranged weapon in inches. See Ranged Combat.

**Reaction**

An interrupting action by a model in Overwatch. See Reactions.

**Ready Model**

A model that has not spent all its ACT and has not chosen PASS. See Turns.

**Region**

The place a battle is fought: which terrain to use and two optional Special Rules. See Regions of Grimheim.

**Roll-off**

A comparison roll with 1D6 each; the higher result wins. See General Principle.

**Save (SAV)**

Save against ranged attacks and normal Strikes: the target number on Defense Dice and on the Armor Save in Close Combat. See Ranged Combat and Close Combat.

**Setup Terms**

The fixed words a Mission uses for terrain, roles, objectives and deployment. See Setup Terms.

**Side Quest**

Optional secondary objectives for additional Victory Points. See Side Quests.

**Status Effect**

A persistent condition on a model. See Status Effects.

**Strike**

Spending a successful Attack Die in Close Combat to deal damage. See Close Combat.

**Structure**

A mission object with SAV and VIT that can be attacked but never attacks; it gives no kill VP. See Mission Rules.

**Tier**

A category of Traits that represents leadership level or experience. See Custom Character Builder.

**Trait**

A special ability of a model (maximum 5 per model). See Model Traits and Weapon Traits.

**Victory Points (VP)**

The points that determine the winner. See Encounter.

**Vitality (VIT)**

The health points of a model. At 0 the model is Defeated. See Vitality.

**Warband**

The group of models belonging to one player. See Building a Warband.

**Weapon Profile**

The statistics of a weapon (RNG, ATK, HTV, DMG, CRT). See The Model Card.
