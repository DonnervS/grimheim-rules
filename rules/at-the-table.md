# At the Table

*Everything you look up during a game, in the order you need it. Nothing about building warbands or campaigns. The reference cards carry the same words.*

## Quick Reference

### Setup

1.  **Mission** · Encounter unless agreed · 600 pts standard · Region and Battle Condition.
2.  **Terrain & objectives** · as the Mission’s Setup Terms say · agree every piece’s type. Encounter: 3 markers in turns · \> 6″ from edges · ≥ 8″ apart.
3.  **Side Quests** · roll D33 twice, keep one.
4.  **Deploy** · as the Mission says, by default alternating. Encounter: Edge Band 6″.

### Turn

1.  **Initiative** · both roll D6, higher activates first, re-roll ties.
2.  **Fate** · roll Fate Dice as D6. ≥ 20 pts cheaper warband: +1 Fate Die.
3.  **Actions** · alternate one model at a time. Out of models: opponent finishes theirs.
4.  **End** · Fate Dice expire · score VP.

**4 turns.** After scoring turn 4: Turn 5 (Overtime) if one player trails by ≥ 5 VP. Never in Stronghold Missions.\
**Outnumbered** · ≥ 2 models fewer at turn start: 1 free OVERWATCH per 2 fewer.

### Actions · same action once per activation

| Action | ACT | Does | Not with |
|----|----|----|----|
| **MOVE** | 1 | up to MOV | Base Contact · RUN · CHARGE · FALL BACK · SHOOT |
| **RUN** | 2 | up to 2 × MOV · counts as MOVE | Base Contact · MOVE · CHARGE · FALL BACK · SHOOT · QUICK SHOT · OVERWATCH |
| **CHARGE** | 1 | MOV + 2″ into Base Contact · +1 ATK · from ≤ 3″ height +1 crit | Base Contact · MOVE · RUN · FALL BACK · HIDE · OVERWATCH |
| **FIGHT** | 1 | close combat as Attacker | only in Base Contact |
| **SHOOT** | 1 | ranged attack | Base Contact · MOVE · RUN · FALL BACK · HIDE · OVERWATCH |
| **QUICK SHOT** | 1 | ranged attack · HTV worsened by 1 · counts as SHOOT | Base Contact · RUN · HIDE · OVERWATCH |
| **FALL BACK** | 2 | move out of Base Contact through enemies | only in Base Contact · MOVE · CHARGE · SHOOT |
| **OVERWATCH** | 1 | ready one Reaction | Base Contact · RUN · CHARGE · SHOOT · QUICK SHOT |
| **HIDE** | 1 | in Cover and out of all enemy LoS: Hidden | Base Contact · SHOOT · CHARGE |
| **INTERACT** | 1 | ≤ 1″: door · reload · loot (once per game per site) · objective · hand over · repeatable | Base Contact |
| **RECOVER** | 1 | remove Stunned · Burning · Frozen · Fearful | Base Contact |
| **PASS** | 1 | nothing · ends the activation early |  |

### Reactions · only on OVERWATCH

1.  Enemy declares an action.
2.  Before dice: one model on Overwatch with a trigger reacts.
3.  Resolve the Reaction, then the enemy action.

**RETREAT** · charged → D3 + 1″ away.\
**DODGE** · shot or spell at you → +1 Defense Die.\
**INTERCEPT** · enemy moves ≤ 2″ → 1 attack, ATK −1, no strike back.\
**REACTION SHOT** · enemy moves ≤ 8″ → shoot, ATK −1, HTV worsened by 1.

No ACT · one per enemy action · never chains · not Stunned or Frozen.

### Close combat

1.  **Engage** one enemy in Base Contact (1″).
2.  **Weapons** · one profile each · off hand 1 ATK · Unarmed 2 / 5+ / 1 / 1.
3.  **Roll** Attack Dice vs HTV and Block Dice. Attacker +1 Attack Die. 6 = crit · 1 = fail.
4.  **Resolve** · one die each, Attacker first:

**STRIKE** · DMG, Armor Save: 1 die vs SAV · crit = CRT + injury roll, no save.\
**PARRY** · Attack Die cancels a success; normal vs normal, crit vs any.\
**BLOCK** · Block Die cancels a success of equal or lower value.

Assist: +1 HTV per extra friend in Base Contact with the Defender (min 2+).

### Ranged combat

1.  **Target** · LoS · in range · ≥ 2″ away · closest legal enemy ≤ 8″ first.
2.  **Attack** · ATK dice vs HTV. Over 8″: HTV worsened by 1 (not spells).
3.  **Defend** · 3 Defense Dice vs SAV (Stalwart +1). Cover: SAV improved by 1.
4.  **Cancel** · crit cancels any · normal cancels normal · 2 normals cancel a crit.
5.  **Damage** · normal = DMG · crit = CRT + injury roll.

Into a melee: re-roll misses, they hit your own model.

### Damage

**VIT 0** · Defeated, removed.\
**Fatal Damage** · no Block, no Armor Save, no Defense roll.\
**Healing** · never above start; full VIT clears injuries.

| D6  | Critical injury                           |
|-----|-------------------------------------------|
| 1   | Head · HTV worsened by 1 (Helmet ignores) |
| 2   | Arm · ATK −1                              |
| 3   | Leg · MOV −1                              |
| 4–6 | Flesh wound                               |

### Status

**Stunned** · only RECOVER or PASS.\
**Burning** · D3 Fatal at turn start.\
**Frozen** · MOV −2″.\
**Fearful** · HTV worsened by 1.\
**Poisoned** · 1 Fatal at turn end.\
**Cursed** · each 1 on Attack or Block Dice: 1 Fatal.

RECOVER: Stunned, Burning, Frozen, Fearful · HEAL: Poisoned · CLEANSE: all six.

### Magic

**CAST A SPELL** · 2D6 = AE · cast cost ≤ AE · each 1 = 2 Fatal to the caster.\
**INVOCATION** · 2D6 = DE · invoke cost ≤ DE · no harm.

Each power once per activation · Scrolls skip the roll.

### Scoring · end of each turn

**1 VP** · per objective you control: a model ≤ 2″ with LoS.\
**+1 VP** · hold it: more of your models control it than enemy models.\
**2 VP** · an enemy Defeated for the first time.\
**Side Quest** · 2 to 6 VP.
