# Custom Character Builder

*The Custom Character Builder lets you design unique models for Grimheim by combining stats, equipment and special abilities. This chapter walks through the build in order and shows what every choice costs.*

## The Build, Step by Step

**At a glance**

- Build in order: Faction, Body Type, Tier, Armor, Traits, Weapons, then optional Equipment.
- Every choice has a points cost. Add them up: that is the model’s cost.
- Body Type gives ACT, MOV and VIT. Armor gives SAV. Every model rolls three Defense Dice.
- A model has no more than 5 Traits. Traits granted by equipment do not count.

Follow the steps below to assemble your model.

1.  **Faction.** Your model’s Faction is determined by the warband it belongs to. The chosen faction grants access to its specific Fate Modifiers and Arcane or Divine Lores. If the warband includes models from more than one faction, it is a Mercenary warband: add 5 points to the cost of every model. This is a faction surcharge, not a trait.
2.  **Body Type.** Select a Body Type to set your model’s base stats: ACT, MOV, VIT, and starting Traits. Some Body Types are exclusive to certain factions. See the relevant faction chapter for the full list.
3.  **Tier.** Choose the Tier to determine whether the model is a standard fighter or an exceptional unit with improved stats and Traits.
4.  **Armor.** Select an Armor type. Armor determines the model’s SAV (Save) value. It grants no Traits.
5.  **Traits.** Define the Class of a model by adding Traits.
6.  **Weapons.** Equip up to two weapons. Weapon profiles define their range, attack values and Traits.
7.  **Equipment** (optional). Add Equipment to enhance your model. Equipment provides Traits and can be found in missions or acquired during a campaign. Traits granted by equipment do not count toward the limit and are not entered in the model’s profile. See Equipment in the Appendix.

**Traits limit and points**

A model can have no more than 5 Traits in total. Traits granted by Body Type or Tier count toward this limit; traits granted by equipment do not count and are not entered in the model’s profile. Weapon traits (the rules printed with a weapon profile) do not. The model’s cost is the sum of everything you chose: Body Type, Tier, Armor, additional Armor Types, Traits, Weapons and Equipment, plus 5 points in a Mercenary warband.

Note the stats in the order of the model card while you build: ACT, MOV, SAV, VIT. Three complete builds follow at the end of this chapter.

## Faction

**At a glance**

- The warband decides the faction of each model.
- The faction decides which Fate Modifier and which Lores you may use.
- Models from more than one faction make a Mercenary warband: +5 points per model, no trait slot used.
- The entries in this chapter are a reduced selection for quick games.

### Faction-specific entries

The entries listed in this section are a reduced selection intended for quick or simplified games. Each faction offers a much broader range of options, including unique entries not shown here. The complete faction-specific choices, including the Fate Modifiers and Lores of single warbands, are kept on grimheim.de (Factions) and in the faction books to come.

Which Fate Modifier and which Lores a Mercenary warband may choose is explained in Building a Warband.

#### Body Type traits in short

Full text in Model Traits in the Appendix.

**Tough:** damage from each resolved normal or critical success is reduced by 1.

**Tougher:** as Tough, but reduced by 2.

**Slow:** cannot RUN or Jump.

**Hardened:** ignore the first Critical Injury each game.

**Fast:** +2″ MOV for CHARGE or FALL BACK.

**Swift:** ignore the climbing movement penalty.

**Lifeless:** immune to Fearful and Poisoned.

**Creature:** cannot INTERACT; cannot be given weapons or equipment, fights only with the natural weapons in its profile.

**Fungus Brew:** may roll a D6 at the start of its activation: 1 Stupidity, 2 to 3 Fearful, 4 to 5 Resilient, 6 Ceaseless, until the end of the turn.

**Furious:** may FIGHT twice per activation.

**Ceaseless:** re-roll Attack Dice results of 1.

**Stupidity:** roll a D6 before an action; on a 1 the action is not performed and the ACT is lost.

## Tier

**At a glance**

- Every model needs a Tier. Standard costs nothing.
- Leader, Hero and Veteran add ACT or VIT and a Trait. Tiers can be combined and add up.
- Limits per warband: 1 Leader, 2 Heroes, 5 Veterans, 2 Arcanists, 1 Archmage.
- Henchmen and Swarm models only keep the Traits of their Body Type.

A model can be a standard unit or it can be an exquisite fighter, like a Leader or a Hero. These models get a bonus to their stats. You have to choose a Tier level for each model. A model can also have multiple Tier levels that add up.

- Only one model of the warband can be selected as Leader and receives the Leader Trait. It is not required to include a Leader or Hero in your warband.
- Up to two models can select the Hero tier.
- Up to 5 models can be Veterans.
- Models with the Henchmen or Swarm tier cannot gain any additional traits beyond those provided by their Body Type.

#### Tier traits in short

**Leader:** may use the COMMAND action.

**Hero:** may use the OVEREXERTION action.

**Veteran:** after finishing its activation, may enter Overwatch without spending ACT; all Overwatch restrictions apply.

**Arcane:** allows the CAST A SPELL action.

**Archmage:** may cast multiple spells per turn using CAST A SPELL.

**Henchmen:** up to 2 models with this trait may activate in succession.

**Swarm:** up to 3 models with this trait may activate in succession.

## Armor

- Choose exactly one Armor type. It sets SAV: none 6+, light 5+, medium 4+, heavy 3+.
- SAV counts against ranged attacks and as Armor Save against normal Strikes in close combat.
- Armor grants no Traits. Full Plate prevents RUN. Helmet, Buckler and Shield are added on top as Traits.

## Traits

- Traits give the model its Class: medic, marksman, scout and so on.
- Count every Trait the model already has before you add more. The maximum is 5.
- Any Model Trait from the Appendix may be added. Some Traits are faction specific.

## Weapons

**At a glance**

- Every model needs at least one weapon to take part in combat. It can carry one or two.
- Hands: one Two-handed weapon, or two one-handed weapons, or a Shield or Buckler plus one one-handed weapon.
- Only weapons with the Two-handed trait are two-handed. All others, ranged weapons included, are one-handed.
- Without a close combat weapon a model fights Unarmed, for free.

Equip the model with one or two Weapons of your choice. Each model needs at least one weapon in order to partake in combat. Use any of these standard weapons or the faction-specific Weapons.

A model can carry:

- one Two-handed weapon, or
- two one-hand weapons, or
- a Shield or Buckler and a one-hand weapon.

Only Two-handed weapons are defined by a Weapon Trait. All others are one-handed.

Remember that a model with the Creature trait cannot be given weapons or equipment. It fights only with the natural weapons listed in its profile.

#### Weapon traits in these tables, in short

Full text in Weapon Traits in the Appendix.

**Ancient:** once per combat, re-roll 1 failed Attack Die.

**Cleave:** if you retain at least one critical success, upgrade one normal success to a critical.

**First Strike:** in Close Combat, this model always resolves the first successful die.

**Master Parry:** a normal success with this weapon may Parry a critical success.

**Riposte:** each Parry with this weapon deals 1 Fatal Damage to the opponent.

**Harmful:** each 1 to hit deals 1 DMG to the Attacker, without defense.

**Two-handed:** no off-hand weapon, Shield or Buckler. Cannot RUN.

**Stun:** a critical success that deals damage also Stuns the target.

**Reach:** can be used in Close and Ranged Combat.

**Impact:** after a CHARGE, deal D3 Fatal Damage to the charged target.

**Snap Shot:** QUICK SHOT with this weapon does not worsen HTV.

**Armor Piercing:** ranged attacks worsen the Defender’s SAV by 1 (min 6+).

**Armor Shatter:** ranged attacks: one Defense Die fewer (min 1) and SAV worsened by 1 (min 6+).

**Reloading:** after a Ranged Attack, reload with INTERACT before firing again.

**Black Powder:** any Attack Die showing 1 means a roll on the Misfire Table; reload with INTERACT after firing.

**Melee Shot:** may SHOOT a target in Base Contact.
