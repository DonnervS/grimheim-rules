# Appendix

*The tables you reach for in the middle of a game. This chapter lists every piece of equipment you can loot and the full rule text of every model trait and weapon trait.*

## Using the Appendix

**At a glance**

- **Looting:** when a model loots a Loot Site, roll a D66 on the Equipment Table and take the item rolled. A result of 11 is a Trap.
- **Traits:** model cards and weapon profiles only name their traits. Look the name up in Model Traits or Weapon Traits. Both lists are alphabetical.
- **Short form and rule:** the italic line under each trait name is the short text printed on the cards. The full text beside it is the rule.
- **Costs:** equipment shows its PTS value for Campaign play. Trait costs are not listed here; they live in the Custom Character Builder.

Nothing in this chapter has to be read front to back. Come here when a model loots, when a card names a trait you do not know, or when two traits meet in the same attack and you need the exact wording.

Items can grant traits (the Boots of Flying grant Fly, the Poison item gives a model's weapons the Poison trait). A model can have no more than 5 Traits in total; traits granted by Body Type or Tier count toward this limit; traits granted by equipment do not count and are not entered in the model’s profile. An item can be handed over to a friendly model within 1″ with INTERACT, and redistributed after the battle.

## Equipment

**At a glance**

- A model within 1″ of a Loot Site can loot it once per game and roll on the Equipment Table.
- Roll a D66: the lower die is the tens, the higher the units.
- On 11 the model finds a Trap and is hit by a Ranged Attack instead of finding an item.
- Every item has a point cost (PTS), its value during Campaign play.

Equipment can be acquired during Missions or throughout a Campaign. Each item has a point cost, representing its value during Campaign play.

- To gain equipment, a model must loot a Loot Site within 1″ and roll on the Equipment Table. Each Loot Site can be looted once per game.
- Roll D66 to determine a random item from the list: the lower die is the tens, the higher die the units.

How looting works during a game (the INTERACT action, which objects count as Loot Sites) is explained in Actions. In a Campaign, looted equipment can be sold for its Gold value (see Campaign Rules).

Example A looting roll goes wrong

The Swordsman (Human: VIT 10; Chainmail, SAV 4+) stands within 1″ of a Loot Site and spends 1 ACT on INTERACT to loot it.

1.  It rolls the D66: \[1\] and \[1\]. The result is **11**: a Trap.
2.  The Trap counts as a Ranged Attack with ATK 3, HTV 4+, DMG 3, CRT 4. Roll its three Attack Dice: \[6\] \[4\] \[2\]. That is one critical success and one normal success.
3.  The Swordsman is the Defender and rolls its 3 Defense Dice against SAV 4+: \[5\] \[3\] \[1\]. One normal success.
4.  A normal Defense success can only discard a normal success, so it cancels the \[4\]. The \[6\] stays.
5.  The critical success deals CRT 4 damage: VIT 10 drops to 6. Because it was a critical hit, the Swordsman rolls on the Critical Injuries Table: \[5\], a Fleshwound with no effect.

The Swordsman finds no item, loses 4 Vitality and has spent the ACT. Had it rolled \[3\] then \[4\], the result 34 would have given it a Lucky Charm.

## Model Traits

**At a glance**

- Model traits are special abilities of the model itself. Its model card names them.
- Many traits unlock an action (CAST A SPELL, COMMAND, HEAL, TAKE AIM …). Without the trait the model cannot use it.
- Buckler and Shield grant Block Dice for Close Combat. Armor itself grants no traits.
- A model can have no more than 5 Traits in total. Traits from equipment do not count.

Example Tough against a crossbow

An Orc Brute (Orc: VIT 12, Tough) is hit by a Crossbow (DMG 3, CRT 4). After defense, one normal success and one critical success remain.

1.  The normal success would deal 3 damage. Tough reduces it by 1: the Orc Brute loses 2 Vitality.
2.  The critical success would deal 4 damage. Tough reduces it by 1: the Orc Brute loses 3 Vitality and rolls for a Critical Injury as normal.

The Orc Brute loses 5 Vitality instead of 7 and drops from 12 to 7. Tough applies to each resolved success separately, not once per attack.

## Weapon Traits

**At a glance**

- Weapon traits belong to one weapon profile and only work when the model attacks with that weapon.
- A weapon can carry several traits. Apply all of them; each one changes only the step it names.
- Many traits trigger on a critical success that inflicts damage (Blighted, Fire, Poison, Stun …).
- Traits such as Reloading, Black Powder and Warp Tech make the weapon unusable until a model reloads it with INTERACT.

Example Two traits on one crossbow

The Crossbowman (Human, ACT 2) carries a Crossbow: RNG 12″, ATK 3, HTV 4+, DMG 3, CRT 4; Armor Piercing, Reloading. It shoots at the Swordsman (Human: VIT 10; Chainmail, SAV 4+), who stands in Cover 7″ away.

1.  The Crossbowman spends 1 ACT on SHOOT. The target is within 8″, so there is no Effective Range penalty.
2.  The Swordsman's SAV: Chainmail 4+. Cover improves it by 1 to 3+. Armor Piercing worsens it by 1, back to 4+. Both modifiers apply.
3.  Attack Dice against HTV 4+: \[6\] \[5\] \[2\]. One critical and one normal success.
4.  Defense Dice against SAV 4+: \[4\] \[3\] \[1\]. One normal success, which discards the normal Attack success. Without Armor Piercing the \[3\] would also succeed, and two normal successes together could have discarded the critical one.
5.  The critical success deals CRT 4: the Swordsman drops from 10 to 6 VIT and rolls on the Critical Injuries Table.
6.  Now Reloading applies: the crossbow cannot fire again until it is reloaded. The Crossbowman spends its second ACT on INTERACT to reload it.

Armor Piercing changes only the Defender's SAV, Reloading only what happens after the shot. Had the Crossbowman spent its second ACT on anything else, the crossbow would stay unloaded, also for an OVERWATCH reaction.
