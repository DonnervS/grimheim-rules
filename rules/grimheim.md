# Grimheim

## What Grimheim Is

Grimheim is a fast skirmish game for a handful of miniatures a side. Players take turns activating one model at a time, so nobody waits long and every decision matters. The rules are free and open, and you may use any miniatures you like.

**A handful of models.** A warband has five to ten fighters, and each of them counts.

**Any miniatures.** Grimheim is miniature agnostic. Use the models you love.

**Little downtime.** Activations alternate model by model, so both players stay in the game.

**Little equipment.** Dice, a ruler, your models and some terrain. No special tokens are required.

**Free warband building.** Build almost any fighter with the Character Builder, or field a ready-made Iconic Warband.

**Tough fighters.** Models are resilient. Fights take several exchanges and reward good timing.

**Free and open.** The rules are free and community driven. Cards and the Companion app at grimheim.app help at the table.

**Print and play.** Model cards, missions, faction rules and campaigns are available as printable files.

## How to Use This Book

**Your first game**

- Read the **At a glance** boxes of chapters I to IV. Together they are the whole game in short form.
- Pick an Iconic Warband or build a few models with chapter VIII, agree on 600 pts, and play the Mission Encounter.
- Look up details when they come up. Every rule has an example, and the Glossary links to the full rule.

## The Game in One Picture

A game of Grimheim is one mission of four turns. In a campaign, the games link up: what your warband wins and loses carries over to the next fight.

1**Build**

your warband from cards or the Character Builder (II, VIII).

2**Play a Mission**

four turns of Initiative, Fate, Actions and End; a fifth in Overtime (III, IV, V).

3**After the battle**

injuries, survivors and Gold from Victory Points (VI).

4**Visit the Hub**

buy, heal, train and recruit, then on to the next Mission (VI).

A single game is step 2. A campaign runs the whole loop again and again.
