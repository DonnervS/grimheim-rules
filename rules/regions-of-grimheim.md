# Regions of Grimheim

*Every Mission is fought somewhere in the ruined city or just beyond its walls. A Region tells you what that place looks like on the table.*

## Using Regions

**At a glance**

- Pick a Region before the game. It lists the terrain pieces to use and how to arrange them.
- Each Region has two optional Special Rules. Agree which you use, if any. They never give Victory Points.
- The Region never changes a Mission’s rules. Each Mission recommends the Regions that suit it best.

A Region tells you which terrain pieces to use, how to arrange them and, if you like, two Special Rules that give it its own character. Agree on the type of each piece before the game (see Terrain).

### Terrain pieces

Sizes are a guide for a 30″ × 30″ battlefield.

| Piece | Footprint | Height | Counts as |
|----|----|----|----|
| **Large building shell** with an upper floor | 6″ × 6″ to 8″ × 10″ | walls 4″ to 6″, floor at 3″ or more | Walls impassable, block Line of Sight; doorways are Entryways; the upper floor is Elevated Ground, reached by ladder or stair. |
| **Tower** | 4″ × 4″ to 8″ × 8″ | 6″ or more | Elevated Ground; Climbing Gear to reach it. |
| **Wall section** | 6″ to 12″ long | over 2″ | Impassable, blocks Line of Sight. |
| **Small ruin** (one room, broken walls) | 3″ × 3″ to 6″ × 6″ | 1″ to 4″ | Walls below model height are Field Defenses; higher walls block Line of Sight. |
| **Rubble hill, crater, grave field** | 4″ to 6″ across | up to 2″ | Difficult Terrain; grants Cover. |
| **Barricade, low wall, fence** | 3″ to 6″ long | below model height | Field Defense (Cover, Traverse 2″). |
| **Scatter:** crates, barrels, stalls, carts, gravestones | 1″ to 3″ | up to 1″ | Small Object (Cover, no movement penalty). |
| **Water, mire, fire bases** | 4″ to 8″ | flat | Difficult or Dangerous Terrain, as the Region says. |

### Terrain density

| Density      | Large  | Medium | Small and scatter |
|--------------|--------|--------|-------------------|
| **Light**    | 1 to 2 | 3 to 4 | 6 to 8            |
| **Standard** | 2 to 3 | 4 to 5 | 8 to 12           |
| **Dense**    | 3 to 4 | 5 to 6 | 12 to 16          |

### Building a good table

- Set up the Mission’s own terrain first, then the Region.
- No open line longer than about 18″ should cross the centre of the table; break long firing lanes with a large or medium piece.
- Leave at least one path 3″ wide between the Deployment Zones, so every warband can reach the fight.
- Give every objective some Cover within 6″, but keep the objective itself reachable: nothing impassable on an Objective Point.
- Two Regions may be mixed; reduce each Region’s pieces by about a third.

## The Broken Streets

Whole streets of timber-framed houses burned out in the faction war. Snipers from every side use the rubble as cover, and in the oldest lanes the ground has fallen in over the tunnels.

Nobody lives here anymore. Somebody always shoots from here anyway.

**Terrain**

2 to 3 large roofless townhouse shells with one upper floor · 3 to 4 small ruins · 2 rubble fans (4″ to 6″) · a lone chimney stack (tall, 1″ × 1″) · doorways barred with stacked planks · one collapse hole (3″ to 4″)

**Layout**

Houses in two broken rows along a bent street; rubble fans spill into the lanes. Put the collapse hole near, but not on, the centre.

**Special Rules · optional**

**Collapse Hole.** The collapse hole is Dangerous Terrain.

**Rooftop Paths.** A model on the upper floor of a ruin may step across a gap of up to 2″ to another upper floor without climbing or jumping.

## Basilica Square

The half-ruined Red Basilica stands over its market, now a hunger bazaar where bread is traded for crystal splinters. The Sun Brothers have walled the precinct into a village of the faithful; before it stands the stone execution podium with its iron rings.

The red windows are broken, but the light still falls red at sunset, and someone still sells bread under them: for crystal, never for coin.

**Terrain**

the broken nave wall of the basilica (large, 10″ long, tall empty window frames) · a stone podium with iron rings (4″ × 4″, 2″ high) · 4 to 6 overturned stalls (Small Objects) · a round well-head (3″) · single-pole banners · a fallen pediment carved with an eight-spoked sun wheel

**Layout**

An open square in the middle, framed by the nave wall on one edge and stalls in loose rows; the podium off-centre.

**Special Rules · optional**

**Hunger Bazaar.** Mark up to two intact stalls as Loot Sites. Each can be looted once per game.

**Public Example.** When a model is Defeated within 3″ of the podium, each friendly model within 6″ of it gains Fearful.

## The Kupferkai

Stone warehouses, quay walls and shipyards along the harbour. Behind linked warehouses lies the Cauldron, a neutral black market where blood is bad for business; everything outside its walls is open ground to fight over.

Tar, fish guts and wet rope. The copper bells don’t ring the hours anymore, only when a crew comes ashore that nobody paid for.

**Terrain**

1 to 2 stone warehouses with iron-bound doors (large) · a quay wall with bollards along one table edge · a crane on a solid timber base (4″ × 4″) · 8 to 12 crates, barrels and sacks · a beached fishing boat (6″ long) · optional: the dark basalt lighthouse stump (tower)

**Layout**

Water only along one short strip at a table edge that is no Deployment Zone; warehouses in two opposite quarters; cargo in loose stacks between.

**Special Rules · optional**

**Harbour Water.** Water is Dangerous Terrain. A model pushed into it is placed at the nearest quay edge after suffering the damage.

**Slippery Quay.** A model pushed while on a quay or jetty is moved 1″ further.

## The Canal Ward

The city canal cuts Grimheim in two. Its bridges are checkpoints or mined, the fish market is no man’s land, and at the Water Gate the Rattlings come in.

Every bridge a toll, every toll a way to die. The canal is no longer a waterway: it is a border.

**Terrain**

the canal (4″ to 6″ wide) across the table between the Deployment Zones · 2 arch bridges (4″ wide, 8″ long) · canal banks with stone facing · stone fish stalls · a gate arch with an iron grille · barricades at the bridgeheads (Field Defenses) · a sunken barge

**Layout**

Run the canal across the middle, parallel to the Deployment Zones, with two bridges at least 12″ apart.

**Special Rules · optional**

**The Canal.** Canal water is Difficult Terrain. A model in the water cannot SHOOT or become Hidden.

**Water Gate.** Models with Ambush may be set up touching the gate arch instead of their normal location.

## The Outer Districts

Beyond the west wall lie the stonemasons’ yards, the beastkin camps, the forges of Forge Row, the tanneries and the slaughterhouse, now a fortified stronghold of the Tribes with stone walls and signal fires.

Soot, lime and blood. The smiths take every order: iron doesn’t ask who will carry it.

**Terrain**

rough timber huts (medium) beside stone barracks (large) · a forge with anvil and quench trough (3″ × 3″) · tanning vats (Small Objects, 2″) · hides hung on single poles · a beastkin shrine of bone and amber · stacks of cut stone blocks (3″ high)

**Layout**

A working yard: forge and vats in the middle third, huts and barracks toward the edges, stone stacks as climbable cover.

**Special Rules · optional**

**Forge Heat.** A model that ends its activation within 1″ of a lit forge suffers 1 Fatal Damage.

**Stone Stacks.** Stacks of cut stone at least 3″ high are Elevated Ground and can be climbed without Climbing Gear.

## The Akademiehöhe

On the highest cliff spur stand the broken halls of the Arkanum: flowing, not-quite-human walls, strange alloys, burst crystal globes. A faint, unnatural violet glimmer lies in the broken stone, the only sign that something below still works.

The Arkanum hums, though no one has studied there in years. At night a violet glimmer sits in the broken stone: too faint to read by, bright enough to fear.

**Terrain**

curved, flowing wall fragments (medium) · toppled column drums (Small Objects) · 2 to 3 dark, burst crystal globes on brass stands (cracked Ley mounts, 2″) · a collapsed lecture hall with tiered seating (large, stepped floors) · a leaning broken tower (tower) · piles of scorched books

**Layout**

Lecture hall and tower in opposite halves; Ley mounts spread so that each is at least 8″ from the others.

**Special Rules · optional**

**Cracked Ley Mounts.** A model with Arcane or Divine generates 1 additional Energy when it rolls for Energy within 3″ of a cracked Ley mount.

**Discharge.** If a model rolls any 1 for Arcane Energy within 3″ of a mount, every other model within 3″ of that mount also suffers 1 Fatal Damage: a pressure wave and heat, no light.

## The Walls

On the land side the south wall is a double wall with a ditch. One section collapsed and was patched with timber and rubble, and every night something tunnels beneath the patch. Small stone watchtowers with signal-fire platforms stand along it.

The south wall is twelve metres high and five thick. Nobody has told the Rattlings, who dig beneath it every night.

**Terrain**

a long wall section with a parapet walkway (12″, walkway at 3″ or more) · a breach patched with timber and rubble (4″ to 6″) · a gatehouse (large) · a small watchtower with signal-fire platform (tower) · a ditch (Difficult Terrain, 2″ wide) · supply stacks behind the wall

**Layout**

The wall runs across the table off-centre, parallel to the Deployment Zones, with the breach and the gate as the two ways through.

**Special Rules · optional**

**Wall Walk.** A model on the parapet walkway is in Cover against Ranged Attacks from the far side of the wall.

**The Patch.** The patched breach is Difficult Terrain; models with Ambush may be set up touching it.

## The Burial Grounds

The city graveyards and the new ones outside the walls are full; the newest graves carry numbers, not names. In the Grove of Remembrance old trees and stone benches stand around a mass grave, and the trees grow wrong.

The newest graves have no names, only numbers. The gravediggers work day and night, and some nights the numbers dig themselves out.

**Terrain**

2 to 3 grave fields of fresh mounds with plain notched stakes (4″ to 6″) · stone tombs (small ruins) · a small flat-roofed mausoleum (medium, roof playable) · open pits beside spoil heaps · stone benches under old trees · a broken boundary wall

**Layout**

Grave fields in rows across the middle, mausoleum off-centre, the wall broken in at least two places.

**Special Rules · optional**

**Open Graves.** Open pits and grave fields are Difficult Terrain.

**Grave Curse.** When a model is Defeated here, place a Curse Marker where it stood. Models within 3″ of it cannot use Fate Dice. A model may remove it with INTERACT.

## The Wasted Fields

South and west of the walls lie vineyards and grain fields, laid waste and only a few still worked. Up the Tyra valley stand evacuated villages with old mills and the ruin of the aqueduct.

The vines still stand in rows. Nobody has pressed wine here in two autumns, and the rows now hide men instead of grapes.

**Terrain**

a burned farmhouse (large) · a stone barn (large) · 2 fields of trampled grain (6″ × 8″) · 2 to 3 vine rows on low stakes (8″ long) · a watermill stump · an aqueduct arch (tall, passable beneath) · dry-stone field walls (Field Defenses)

**Layout**

Buildings in two opposite quarters, vine rows and walls at angles (L and T shapes, no long corridors).

**Special Rules · optional**

**Vine Rows.** A model within vine rows is in Cover against Ranged Attacks, whatever the angle.

**Furrowed Ground.** RUN and CHARGE across ploughed fields move 2″ less.

## The Silent Lines

West of the walls runs Siebenbürgen’s siege ring: ditches, palisades and silent tents without fires, because the dead need neither light nor warmth.

No fires, no songs, no smell of cooking. The siege lines cost the dead nothing. They cost the city everything.

**Terrain**

1 to 2 trench lines with plank walkways (12″, angled) · sharpened palisades (Field Defenses) · rows of silent dark tents (Small Objects, 3″) · a parley tent with carpets (medium) · earthworks (Field Defenses) · cold fire pits

**Layout**

Trenches angled across the table, tents behind them, open ground between the lines.

**Special Rules · optional**

**Prepared Positions.** A model wholly within a trench is in Cover against Ranged Attacks from its front, not from above or behind.

**Staked Approaches.** Lines of sharpened stakes are Dangerous Terrain.
